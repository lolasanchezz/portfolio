one day, i had a surplus of claude credits, very little to do, and a lot of boredom. so i decided to ask claude to look at my imessages and make some interesting data visualizations. 

it was okay - i didn't learn that much about my existing relationships that i knew already. but i did see the breadth of my messages. my apple account kept messages from the past year, which was 63,000 texts, 28,261 from me. these were all kept neatly on sqlite databases on my computer, free to access.

obviously, when you find a healthy, interesting, large data source, there are two paths you can take - make a data visualization of it, which i already did, or train a model off of it. and there's nothing a LLM likes more than conversational, text data.
